# Electrochemical calculations

- **Reference scales:** Experimental conversions live in `reference_electrodes.py`. Require the exact electrode/filling-solution variant and temperature. `E_RHE = E_SHE + (RT/F) ln(10) pH` under the stated Nernst assumptions; do not apply a second pH shift to RHE data. Record electrolyte activities/temperature assumptions when needed.
- **Signed currents and resistance:** Preserve anodic-positive/cathodic-negative convention after verifying instrument settings. `E_corrected = E_measured - I * Ru * fraction` uses amperes and ohms, not mA/cm2. `get_ir_corrected_potential(ru_ohm=..., compensation_fraction=...)` refuses a dataset marked compensated, but returns an array without marking new state. Record the correction externally or in an appropriate derived dataset.
- **CV/LSV:** Use `cv.find_cv_peaks` / `integrate_cv_charge` and `lsv.extract_overpotential_at_current`. Define background, integration boundaries, direction, cycle, and normalization. Charge integrates current against time; potential-domain integration requires scan rate and sign treatment. HER/OER equilibrium reference and reaction direction must be explicit. Do not extrapolate target currents outside measured coverage without clearly identifying extrapolation.
- **Tafel:** `tafel.fit_tafel_slope` uses a selected kinetic range; report log base, current units, slope sign/magnitude, residuals and uncertainty. Its `NonSteadyStateWarning` threshold is a diagnostic, not evidence that lower scan rates are steady state. Exclude transport-limited, capacitive, and resistance-dominated regions with justification.
- **Cdl/ECSA:** Reuse `ecsa.extract_cdl_from_scan_rates`, `calculate_double_layer_capacitance`, and `estimate_ecsa` as appropriate to their signatures. State whether half the anodic/cathodic current difference was used, area normalization, and the assumed specific capacitance with source/uncertainty. Preserve `CdlInterpretationWarning`.
- **EIS:** `eis.fit_equivalent_circuit` and `run_kramers_kronig_test` use impedance.py when available. Check frequency units/order, complex sign, stationarity, perturbation amplitude, fit bounds, residuals, and parameter correlations. Lin-KK consistency is a data diagnostic, not circuit uniqueness: [method/API documentation](https://impedancepy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/validation.html).
- **CA/CP and benchmarking:** Use the stability metric functions in `chronoamperometry.py` / `chronopotentiometry.py` with explicit transient exclusions, duration, and normalization; gas bubbles, drift, and catalyst loss can confound interpretation. `benchmarking.compare_instrument_lsv` requires matched conditions and common measured coverage.
- **CHE:** `modules.catalysis.reaction_energy.apply_che_correction` defines `nu_H > 0` for consumed proton/electron pairs (reduction) and `< 0` for produced pairs. In eV, the implementation adds `nu_H * U_RHE`, or `nu_H * (U_SHE + k_B T ln(10) pH)`. Passing `nu_H` overrides `step_type` and `n_electrons`. Check a signed one-step example and equivalent SHE/RHE potentials. Reference: [Norskov et al., 2004, Origin of the Overpotential for Oxygen Reduction at a Fuel-Cell Cathode](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp047349j). Record gas reference, ZPE/entropy terms, and model limitations separately from measured kinetics.
