# Energy and reference conventions

- Reuse `scripts.preprocess.convert_energy` and its named constants. The repository intentionally distinguishes `RY_TO_EV_QE` from `RY_TO_EV_CODATA2018`; do not replace one with the other silently. Check the producing QE version's constants when precision matters. Total `pw.x` energies are Ry; DOS, PDOS, and Gnuplot band energies are already eV.
- Keep eV per particle/system distinct from kJ/mol and kcal/mol. State whether energy is per cell, adsorbate, formula unit, or reaction. Verify force and length conversions together, and distinguish fractional from Cartesian coordinates.
- Fermi alignment subtracts a specified `E_F` exactly once. Record its originating calculation, smearing/spin context, and original energy axis. Several local alignment APIs return unshifted data if no Fermi energy exists; the caller must label that honestly.
- `modules.dft.adsorption.calculate_adsorption_energy` computes `(E_total - E_clean_surface - E_adsorbate_ref) / n_adsorbates`, with selectable sign convention. Its reference argument must include the correct total stoichiometric reference energy for the chosen numerator. State molecular/atomic/gas reference, spin state, normalization, and favorable sign. Check compatible functional, pseudopotentials, cell, cutoffs, and sampling; a numerical difference alone is insufficient.
- `adapters.calculate_adsorption_energy_from_qe` checks the slab calculations' validity but accepts a numeric reference energy. The reference calculation's convergence and physical suitability remain the caller's responsibility.
- Distinguish electronic adsorption energies, ZPE/entropy-corrected free energies, and electrochemical CHE shifts. Use the [electrochemical conventions](../../electrochemistry/references/electrochemical-conventions.md) for SHE/RHE and signed stoichiometry. Do not borrow an unsigned CHE formula from historical narrative documentation.
